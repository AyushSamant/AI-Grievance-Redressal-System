HEALTH DEPARTMENT (Scope and Examples)

Scope:
Handles complaints related to hospitals, clinics, medical services, emergency response, and healthcare facilities.

Examples:
- Ambulance not responding
- Doctor unavailable in government hospital
- Emergency medical support required
- Poor hygiene in hospital ward
- Denial of treatment or delay in care

Required Proof (recommended):
- Hospital slip or report
- Medical documents if available
- Voice note describing emergency situation

Priority Guide:
- CRITICAL: life-threatening emergencies, accident cases, severe medical risk
- HIGH: hospital not responding, emergency services delayed
- MEDIUM/LOW: general service complaints, minor healthcare facility issues
