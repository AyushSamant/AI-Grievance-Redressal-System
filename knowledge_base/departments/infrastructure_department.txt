INFRASTRUCTURE DEPARTMENT (Scope and Examples)

Scope:
Handles public infrastructure problems such as roads, bridges, streetlights, construction hazards, and maintenance.

Examples:
- Potholes / broken roads causing accidents
- Streetlight not working
- Damaged footpath or open manhole
- Unsafe construction debris on road
- Bridge cracks or structural damage

Recommended Proof:
- Photos/videos with location details
- Time and date info
- Nearby landmark reference

Priority Guide:
- HIGH/CRITICAL: hazards causing accidents or life risk
- MEDIUM: maintenance required but no immediate danger
- LOW: minor repair requests
