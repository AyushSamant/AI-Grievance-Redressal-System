WATER SUPPLY DEPARTMENT (Scope and Examples)

Scope:
Handles drinking water issues, supply interruptions, pipeline leaks, low pressure, and contamination.

Examples:
- No water supply for hours/days
- Pipeline leakage near home
- Dirty or contaminated water
- Low pressure during peak hours

Proof:
- Photo/video of leakage
- Short voice note describing duration and impact
- Address and landmark details

Priority Guide:
- HIGH: no water supply, contamination risk
- MEDIUM: intermittent supply, low pressure
- LOW: minor complaints, general requests
