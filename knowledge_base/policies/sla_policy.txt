SLA POLICY (Service Level Agreement)

Purpose:
SLA ensures complaints are resolved within an expected time window based on urgency and public impact.

SLA Targets by Priority:
- CRITICAL: 2 days (emergency and safety-related issues)
- HIGH: 4 days (serious issues impacting daily life)
- MEDIUM: 7 days (important but not immediate emergency)
- LOW: 14 days (non-urgent requests and minor issues)

Notes:
- SLA indicates target resolution time, not guaranteed completion.
- Departments should provide updates for delays and reasons.
- High urgency complaints should be prioritized in officer queues.

Examples:
- CRITICAL: Accident emergency, fire hazard, major violence threat
- HIGH: No water supply, major power issues, broken roads causing accidents
- MEDIUM: Local sanitation issue, minor infrastructure repair
- LOW: Requests such as information, minor maintenance
