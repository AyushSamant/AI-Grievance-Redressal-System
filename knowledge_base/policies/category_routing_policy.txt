CATEGORY ROUTING POLICY

Purpose:
To ensure complaints are routed to the correct authority automatically and consistently.

Category to Department Mapping:
- HEALTH -> Health Department
- INFRASTRUCTURE -> Infrastructure Department
- WATER -> Water Supply Department
- ELECTRICITY -> Electricity Department
- SANITATION -> Sanitation Department
- AGRICULTURE -> Agriculture Department
- EDUCATION -> Education Department
- PUBLIC_SAFETY -> Public Safety Department

Routing Rules:
1) AI predicts category using complaint description + title.
2) System assigns department based on mapping.
3) If department is inactive or missing, complaint is marked for manual review by Admin.
